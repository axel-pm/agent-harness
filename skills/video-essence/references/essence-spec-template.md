# essence-spec-template.md

The portable spec to produce from a watched reference. Source-anchored (every claim cites a
frame timestamp or transcript span), medium-agnostic (describes the *feel*, not the topic).

```md
# Essence: <a short name for this look/feel>
Source: <title / URL>  ·  Duration: <s>  ·  Watched: <N frames, transcript y/n>

## One-line essence
The single sentence a stranger could use to recreate the feel.

## Visual style
- Palette: … (evidence: frame #_ @ _s)
- Typography: …
- Composition: …
- Texture/treatment: …

## Motion & pacing
- Cut rhythm: …
- Transitions: …
- Element motion / easing: …
- Energy curve: …   (evidence: frames @ _ , _ , _)

## Structure & beats
1. <beat> — ~<t>s — <what happens>
2. …
- Hook: …   Ending: …

## Tone & voice
- Mood / register / rhetoric: …   (evidence: transcript [_–_])

## Signature technique
The 1–2 moves that make it recognizable: …

## Audio / music
- … or N/A

## Do-not-transfer
The source's actual content/subject/assets that must NOT carry over (this is what keeps the
transfer original, not a copy).
```

Keep it tight. If a dimension is N/A, say so — a spec full of honest N/As is more useful than one
padded with guesses.
