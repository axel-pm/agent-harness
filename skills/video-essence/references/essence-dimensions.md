# essence-dimensions.md — what to look for, and how to read it

The transferable qualities of a video, and where each shows up in a digest. Not every dimension
applies to every video — capture the salient ones, mark the rest N/A.

## 1. Visual style
The static look of any single frame.
- **Palette** — dominant + accent colors, saturation, contrast, light vs dark.
- **Typography** — serif/sans/mono, weight, size hierarchy, letter-spacing, all-caps vs sentence.
- **Composition** — grid vs centered, dense vs airy, margins, alignment, focal point.
- **Texture / treatment** — flat, gradient, grain, glow, shadow, hand-drawn, photographic.
*Read from:* individual frames (full-res). Compare 2–3 frames to separate constants (the style) from content.

## 2. Motion & pacing
How it moves and how fast.
- **Cut rhythm** — long holds vs rapid cuts; regular vs syncopated.
- **Transitions** — hard cut, dissolve, wipe, morph, match-cut; their frequency.
- **Element motion** — easing (snappy vs floaty), direction, parallax, kinetic type.
- **Energy curve** — where it accelerates / rests.
*Read from:* the sequence of frames + their timestamps (spacing of change), and the contact sheet's arc. Fast-changing scene-mode frames cluster where motion is busy.

## 3. Structure & beats
The skeleton of how it's organized in time.
- **Hook** — how the first few seconds grab.
- **Arc** — setup → development → payoff; number and order of beats.
- **Beat timing** — roughly how long each section runs.
- **Ending** — CTA, loop, button, fade.
*Read from:* transcript segmentation + frame changes at boundaries.

## 4. Tone & voice
The register and personality.
- **Mood** — playful, austere, urgent, warm, technical, cinematic.
- **Register** — formal/casual, expert/friendly.
- **Rhetoric** — question-led, claim-then-proof, story, list, demo.
- **Humor / restraint** — where it leans in or holds back.
*Read from:* transcript (word choice, sentence length, rhythm) + how visuals reinforce it.

## 5. Signature technique
The one or two moves that make it recognizable — the thing you'd point at and say "that's the trick."
- A recurring visual device, a specific transition it repeats, a structural gimmick, a distinctive pace.
*Read from:* whatever repeats across the digest. If you can name the signature move, you can transfer it.

## 6. Audio / music
- **Music** — genre, tempo, energy, how it's synced to cuts.
- **VO cadence** — pace, pauses, emphasis.
- **Sound design** — whooshes, clicks, stingers on transitions.
*Read from:* transcript timing + (if present) `notes`. N/A for silent references.

## Separating essence from content
Ask of every observation: *"Is this how it's made, or what it's about?"* Keep the **how** (transferable),
discard the **what** (the source's specific subject). The essence spec should read as a recipe you could
apply to a totally different topic.
